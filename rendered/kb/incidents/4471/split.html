Fixing the parser closes only the first row. The other two need their own fixes.

  • parser: the shared parser reads dates month first; every tenant on file writes the day first. “7 April” comes out as “4 July”. This is the bug #4471 describes.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix does not touch it.
  • shift: the booking lands one day off, or on a different day for people in different time zones. The month never moves. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but the table filed them under parser by tenant. This looks like a time-zone conversion bug.

Outcome

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October:

  • parser: fixed, fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. #1 closed as completed.
  • hint: won’t fix, the booking-v3 form is retired. #2 closed as not planned.
  • shift: the desk files these reports under parser by tenant. #3 closed as a duplicate of #1. If one-day moves keep coming in after the parser fix, reopen it.

How the reports were placed

  • Count each report once. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Every count here is by unique report id.
  • Place by tenant, not by intake tag. The intake tag is guessed from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts, locales.ts).
  • Read before merging. A matching subject says two people chose the same words, not that they hit the same bug. Reports with the same subject sit in all three rows.
